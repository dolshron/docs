---
title: 'GET /organization/products/:id — Get Product Details'
sidebarTitle: 'Get'
description: 'Fetch the full record for a single product by ID, including name, price, currency, billing type, product status, and any stored metadata.'
---

Use this endpoint to retrieve the complete details of a specific product — for example, to confirm a product's price and currency before attaching it to an invoice, or to display product information in your merchant dashboard. Pass the product `id` as a path parameter.

## Path parameters

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier of the product to retrieve, e.g. `prod_01hw3x7k2r4p5q6m8n9v`.
</ParamField>

```bash
curl --request GET \
  --url https://core.nadapay.io/api/v1/organization/products/prod_01hw3x7k2r4p5q6m8n9v \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY'
```

## Response

A `200` response returns the full product object.

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">
  The product's unique identifier.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="name" type="string">
  The product name.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="description" type="string">
  The product description.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="sku" type="string">
  The product's stock-keeping unit or code.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="type" type="string">
  The billing type: `ONE_TIME` or `RECURRING`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="price" type="number">
  The product price in micro-units.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="currency" type="string">
  The product currency code.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="billingPeriod" type="string">
  The billing cycle for recurring products.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
  The product status: `ACTIVE`, `DRAFT`, or `ARCHIVED`.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="isActive" type="boolean">
  Whether the product is currently active.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">
  ISO 8601 creation timestamp.
</ResponseField>
