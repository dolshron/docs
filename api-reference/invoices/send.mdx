---
title: 'POST /organization/invoices/:id/send — Send Invoice to Customer'
sidebarTitle: 'Send'
description: 'Transition a DRAFT invoice to SENT status and email it to the customer. This action is irreversible — the invoice becomes immutable after sending.'
---

When you are ready to deliver an invoice to your customer, call this endpoint. NadaPay transitions the invoice from `DRAFT` to `SENT`, dispatches an email to the customer with a link to the invoice payment page, and locks the invoice from further edits. If you need to make changes, do so while the invoice is still in `DRAFT` before calling this endpoint. You can also send automatically at creation time by setting `sendNow: true` on the [Create Invoice](/api-reference/invoices/create) request.

## Path parameters

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
  The unique identifier of the draft invoice to send.
</ParamField>

```bash
curl --request POST \
  --url https://core.nadapay.io/api/v1/organization/invoices/inv_abc123/send \
  --header 'x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY'
```

## Response

A `200` response confirms the invoice was sent and returns the updated invoice object with `status: "SENT"`.

<ResponseField name="id" type="string">
  The invoice's unique identifier.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="invoiceNumber" type="string">
  The customer-facing invoice number.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="status" type="string">
  The invoice status after sending. Always `SENT` on success.
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="sentAt" type="string">
  ISO 8601 timestamp of when the invoice was sent.
</ResponseField>

```json
{
  "success": true,
  "statusCode": 200,
  "message": "Invoice sent successfully",
  "data": {
    "id": "inv_abc123",
    "invoiceNumber": "INV-001",
    "status": "SENT",
    "sentAt": "2026-04-24T12:00:00.000Z"
  }
}
```

<Note>
  After this call succeeds, the customer receives an email with a link to `/public/invoices/:number` where they can view and pay the invoice. The invoice can no longer be edited or deleted.
</Note>
