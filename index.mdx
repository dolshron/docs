Everything you need to accept money, move money, and scale across global markets is available through a single, unified API.

## Get Started in 4 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your API key">
    Sign up for a NadaPay account and retrieve your API key from the dashboard. Use `np_test_...` keys for sandbox and `np_live_...` keys for production.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate your requests">
    Pass your key in the `x-api-key` header on every request. See [Authentication](/docs/authentication) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your integration path">
    Collect inbound payments with [Checkout](/docs/accept-payments) or [Payment Links](/docs/payment-links), or send outbound payouts using the [Payout API](/docs/payout-overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Swap in your `np_live_...` key, complete KYB verification, and start processing real transactions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
